    Privacy, in plain words

    Last updated October 1, 2026

    What we keep

    • Your account: your email address, password (stored securely by our database provider, never readable by us) and your cook name.
    • Your kitchen: which recipes you drew and when, your star ratings and kitchen notes, recipes you saved, and challenges you joined.
    • Dish photos you choose to upload. Only you can see them.
    • Feedback you send, plus your email only if you type it in so we can reply.

    Your star ratings are combined with everyone else's to make the community rating. Other people never see your name, notes or photos.

    Where it lives

    Accounts and data are stored with Supabase, our database provider. In demo mode, everything stays in your own browser and never leaves your device. Your theme, units and filter choices are always remembered only in your browser.

    What we don't do

    • No ads, no selling or sharing your data, and no tracking or advertising cookies.
    • No analytics.
    • We only email you for account things you ask for, like confirming your address or resetting your password.

    Other sites involved

    Fonts load from Google Fonts, and recipe photos load from the sites they came from (such as TheMealDB and Wikimedia Commons). Like any website, those servers can see your IP address when your browser fetches the files. If the sign-up form shows a spam check, it comes from Cloudflare Turnstile, which doesn't use tracking cookies. Links to original recipes take you to other sites with their own privacy policies.

    Your choices

    You can sign out at any time. To have your account and everything in it deleted, send a request through the Feedback page with the email you signed up with, and it will be removed.

    About Find Your Recipe

    Bored of cooking the same five things? Stir the pot, get a random recipe from somewhere in the world, cook it, and only then find out whether everyone else thought it was a 1-star flop or a 5-star feast.

    How it works

    • 3 stirs a day. No rerolling until you find something safe. That's the gamble.
    • The rating stays hidden until you've cooked the dish and rated it yourself, so nobody can cherry-pick the good ones.
    • Picky eater mode lets you filter by culture, time, diet and allergies if you need to.
    • A weekly challenge gives everyone the same mystery recipe.

    Where the recipes come from

    Thousands of recipes from open, free-to-share sources: TheMealDB, the Wikibooks Cookbook, public-domain recipe collections and classic cookbooks from the 1800s on Project Gutenberg. Every recipe links back to where it came from. See Credits & licences for the details.

    Who's behind it

    Find Your Recipe is an independent project. It has no ads and doesn't sell your data. Questions, ideas or problems? Use the Feedback page. Every message is read.

    • Allergens and diet tags are automatic guesses made from ingredient names. They can be wrong or incomplete. If you have an allergy or medical diet, always read the full ingredient list and check product labels.
    • Vintage recipes (marked 📜) are historical, from the 1800s. Some of their methods and ingredients are no longer considered safe. Check modern guidance before following their advice on saltpetre, raw eggs, home curing, preserving or canning.
